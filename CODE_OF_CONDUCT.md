# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Bullpen to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background, identity, experience level or how they like to work. We pledge to keep participation free of harassment.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Disagree about ideas, not about people. Give feedback that is specific and useful.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure what someone meant.
- Respect other people's time and effort, including volunteer maintainers.
- Keep discussion on topic: the project, its code and its users.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Spam, or repeatedly pressuring maintainers.
- Anything else that a reasonable person would find hostile in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may restrict participation for people who repeat or seriously break them.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on their GitHub profile ([@VenkateshRnb](https://github.com/VenkateshRnb)). Reports are handled with discretion, and reporters will not be punished for good-faith reports.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.
